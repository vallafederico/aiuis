# Brand voice — aiu.is

## The short version

Direct. Curious. A practitioner writing for other practitioners. Never performing excitement. Never hedging to seem neutral.

## Tone

Write as someone who built the thing and has opinions about it — not as someone explaining it to a beginner, and not as someone selling it. The reader is technical. They will notice if you simplify past the truth.

Show curiosity about trade-offs. When a decision was close, say so. When something surprised you, name the surprise. When something did not work, be specific about why.

Do not announce what you are about to say. Cut the preamble.

## Terminology

Use "agent" not "AI agent" — once the context is established, the modifier adds nothing.

Use "markdown" (lowercase) as a noun and adjective.

Use "worker" (lowercase) when referring to a Cloudflare Worker in running context. "Workers" (capital) for the Cloudflare product/platform.

Prefer concrete nouns over abstract ones: "the D1 migration" not "the data layer change".

## Length

Posts: 300–1500 words. If a post runs over 1500 words without a clear reason, it should probably be two posts.

Paragraphs: 2–4 sentences. Single-sentence paragraphs are fine when the sentence is doing enough work.

## What to avoid

Summaries that restate the intro. A conclusion that says "In this post, I covered..." is not a conclusion, it is padding — cut it or replace with one forward-looking sentence.

Vague gestures at difficulty ("this was tricky", "surprisingly complex") without specifics. Name what was hard.
