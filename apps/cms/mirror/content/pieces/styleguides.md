A styleguide for an AI-augmented product has to account for outputs it did not author. This chapter proposes a framework for extending design tokens and component libraries to cover generated content—text, images, citations—without surrendering visual coherence.

:::notes
Examples draw from production systems where the generated/designed boundary became a live design problem.
:::
