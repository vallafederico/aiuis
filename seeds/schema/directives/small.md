# `:::small` directive guidelines

Use `:::small` for fine print that belongs where it sits in the text rather than at the end: image and asset credits, third-party licenses, short disclaimers. The site renders it at the notes size, without a label, in reading order.

Each line of the body becomes its own line of fine print. A line that is only a link stays a link.

Use as many `:::small` blocks as the document needs. Endnote material and the reference list still go in `:::notes`.

No attributes are accepted. The body is standard markdown.
