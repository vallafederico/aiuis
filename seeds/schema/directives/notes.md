# `:::notes` directive guidelines

Use `:::notes` for material secondary to the argument: affiliation disclosures, methodology caveats, contextual asides, and the reference list. The site renders it as a visually distinct block below the main body, outside the table of contents.

The reference list follows the brand-voice skill. One work per line: author, title, year, and the journal or publisher when the title is ambiguous. Every work cited in the body appears here. Do not invent a source.

One `:::notes` block per document, at the end of the body. If you find yourself wanting a second one, the content belongs inline or in a separate section heading.

No attributes are accepted. The body is standard markdown.
