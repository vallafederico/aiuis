# `:::foreword` directive guidelines

Use `:::foreword` for a brief lead-in that sits between the title and the article body: one or two sentences that orient the reader before the piece begins. It is not a restatement of the opening paragraph — write a short teaser, not a duplicate.

Optional. At most one `:::foreword` block per document, and it must appear at the start of the body, before any other content.

No attributes are accepted. The body is standard markdown.
