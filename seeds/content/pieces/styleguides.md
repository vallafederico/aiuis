A styleguide for an AI-augmented product has to account for outputs it did not author. The framework proposed here extends design tokens and component libraries to cover generated content (text, images, citations) without giving up visual coherence.

:::notes
Examples come from production systems where the boundary between generated and designed became a live design problem.
:::
