The changelog names each UI added to aiu.is and the date it landed. It records the implementation on the page, not the site chrome around it.

21 September 2026. FAQs. The Ask row searches the published chapters. A match opens the existing answer. A miss the index covers becomes a new row that cites its chapter. Silence is the honest miss.

21 September 2026. Find. A query lights paragraphs by meaning across the published chapters, or leaves them dark. The hit is the highlighted passage.

21 September 2026. Navigation. Search reorders the catalog in the page. Soft chips cut scope when a TypeSafe key is present. A weak match leaves the order alone.

22 September 2026. Infinite Article. At the end of a section the page offers a fork: continue, deepen, aside, or stop. Generation runs only after a direction is chosen. A weak engagement gate stays quiet.

22 September 2026. Images. Search ranks a caption and prompt grid in place. Soft chips cut by provenance when a TypeSafe key is present. Prompt-match scores order and cut; nothing new is generated.

22 September 2026. Generative Moodboard. Two named axes place tiles by score. A click in empty space mixes or varies toward that point; a dense cluster stays quiet. Generation shows its sources and weights.

22 September 2026. Image Generation. A prompt and a result, then iterate, vary, or stop. Sliders surface from the prompt. Stopping is a first-class act.
