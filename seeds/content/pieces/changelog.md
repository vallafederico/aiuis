Each UI added to aiu.is is listed here with the date it landed. The entry records what the page implements, not the site chrome around it.

21 September 2026. FAQs. The Ask row searches the published chapters. A match opens the existing answer. A miss the index covers becomes a new row that cites its chapter. Silence is the honest miss.

21 September 2026. Find. A query lights paragraphs across the published chapters by meaning, or leaves them dark. The hit is the highlighted passage.

21 September 2026. Navigation. Search reorders the catalog on the page. With a TypeSafe key present, soft chips cut scope. A weak match leaves the order alone.

22 September 2026. Infinite Article. At the end of a section, the page offers a fork: continue, deepen, aside, or stop. Generation runs only once a direction is chosen. A weak engagement gate stays quiet.

22 September 2026. Images. Search ranks a caption and prompt grid in place. With a TypeSafe key present, soft chips cut by provenance. Prompt-match scores order and cut; nothing new is generated.

22 September 2026. Generative Moodboard. Two named axes place tiles by score. A click in empty space mixes or varies toward that point; a dense cluster stays quiet. Generation shows its sources and weights.

22 September 2026. Image Generation. A prompt and a result, then iterate, vary, or stop. Sliders surface from the prompt. Stopping is a first-class act.
