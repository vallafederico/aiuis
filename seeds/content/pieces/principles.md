Six principles for designing AI interfaces, derived inductively from the case studies in this thesis: legibility, interruptibility, recoverability, attribution, calibration, and restraint. Each is defined precisely enough to fail—vague principles are not principles.

:::notes
The principles are ordered by the phase of an interaction they most affect, from first contact to long-term use.
:::
